## Overview

At **Mappingr**, we respect your right to privacy and give you full control over your personal data. In compliance with Google Play Store policies, users of Mappingr can easily request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

You can delete your account either directly **within the Mappingr mobile application** or **via email without needing the app installed**.

---

## How to Request Account Deletion

### Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)
If you have the Mappingr app installed on your device:
1. Open the **Mappingr** app.
2. Tap on your **Profile icon** (bottom navigation bar).
3. Scroll down to the **Account & Privacy** section.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Confirm your choice when prompted. Your account session will terminate immediately and your data will be queued for deletion.

---

### Method 2: Web / Email Request (Without App Installed)
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request full account deletion via email:
1. Send an email to: **[dhruvgaur.in@gmail.com](mailto:dhruvgaur.in@gmail.com)**
2. Use the subject line: `[Mappingr] Account Deletion Request`
3. In the body of the email, please provide:
   - Your **registered phone number** (including country code, e.g., `+91 XXXXX XXXXX`) used during login.
   - Your display name (optional, helps verify identity).
4. Our support team will process your deletion request and confirm once complete.

---

## What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

When your account deletion is processed, the following personal data is **permanently purged** from our Supabase database and cloud storage:

- **Authentication Data:** Your registered phone number and user ID.
- **Profile Information:** Your display name, profile avatar/photo, and bio.
- **Activity & Meetup Records:** All activity pins, hangouts created, and meetup participation history.
- **In-App Messages:** All live chat messages sent across all activity rooms.
- **Location Information:** Any cached or stored GPS coordinates associated with your user session.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Firebase notification tokens tied to your device.

---

## What Data Is Retained (If Any) & Retention Period

- **No Personal Data Retained:** We do not retain any identifiable personal information, phone numbers, or chat logs after account deletion.
- **Technical Logs:** Non-identifying, aggregated server logs (e.g., error timestamps) may be retained for up to **30 days** solely for security, fraud prevention, and server debugging, after which they are automatically rotated and overwritten.
- **Processing Time:** All deletion requests submitted via email are verified and fulfilled within **30 calendar days**.
