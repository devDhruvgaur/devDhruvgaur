## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Mappingr** ("we", "our", or "us"). Mappingr is an urban activity discovery and live hangouts mobile application that connects people through real-world meetups, real-time satellite maps, and group chat.

We value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it is processed and shared, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Mappingr mobile application.

By installing or using Mappingr, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data (Foreground Only)
- **What We Collect:** Approximate and precise geographic coordinates (GPS and network-based location).
- **How We Use It:** To center the satellite map on your current area, show nearby activities and hangouts, calculate proximity to events, and allow you to drop hangout pins at your chosen location.
- **Foreground Notice:** We only access your device location **while the application is actively running in the foreground**. We do **NOT** access, track, or record your background location when the app is closed or minimized.
- **User Control:** You can enable, disable, or modify location permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

### B. Account & Authentication Information
- **Phone Number:** Collected when you sign in via Phone OTP (One-Time Password) to verify your identity and protect against automated spam/abuse.
- **Profile Information:** Your display name, bio/interests, and optional profile avatar that you choose to provide.

### C. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Activity Data
- **Hangout Pins:** Meetup titles, descriptions, categories (e.g., sports, social, study, casual), times, and pinned location coordinates.
- **Activity Participation:** Records of activities you create, host, or join as a participant.
- **In-App Chat Messages:** Real-time messages sent within hangout chat rooms to coordinate meetups with other participants.

### D. Device and Technical Information
- Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, network connection status (Wi-Fi/cellular), and crash logs to maintain app stability, prevent errors, and optimize performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:
1. **Core App Functionality:** Providing interactive satellite maps, pin placement, real-time meetup discovery, and live chat.
2. **Account Management:** Authenticating users securely via phone verification without requiring complex passwords.
3. **Safety & Moderation:** Enforcing community guidelines, preventing spam, investigating harassment reports, and honoring user blocks.
4. **Platform Improvements:** Diagnosing bugs, optimizing network payloads, and improving map rendering speed.

## 5. User Safety, Moderation & Reporting

Mappingr provides built-in tools to ensure a safe and friendly community:
- **Reporting System:** Users can immediately report inappropriate content, spam, or hostile activities directly from the activity card or user profile.
- **Blocking Mechanism:** Users can block any participant at any time. Blocked users will not see your activities and you will not see theirs.
- **Host Controls:** Activity creators can edit or delete their hosted activities at any time.

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

We respect your right to control your personal data.

### How Long We Keep Data:
- Active hangout pins automatically expire or are archived after the event finishes.
- Account information is retained as long as your account remains active.

### How to Request Account and Data Deletion:
In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time:
1. **In-App:** Go to your **Profile / Settings** screen inside the Mappingr app and tap **Delete Account**.
2. **Via Email:** Send an email from your registered phone number or identity to **[dhruvgaur.in@gmail.com](mailto:dhruvgaur.in@gmail.com)** with the subject line `Account Deletion Request - Mappingr`.

**What Happens Upon Deletion:**
Within 30 business days of receiving your request, we permanently purge:
- Your user profile (name, avatar, bio, phone number).
- All chat messages authored by your account.
- All activity pins created or joined by your account.
- Any cached server identifiers.

## 7. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All network communication between the Mappingr client and cloud servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS protocols.
- **Row-Level Security (RLS):** Database queries are governed by strict database policies ensuring users can only read authorized data and edit their own profiles.
- **Token-based Authentication:** Secure session tokens with automatic expiry.
